Who is this for?

Founders, corporate leaders, therapists and coaches — 35 to 55 — who are excellent at what they do and go quiet when it's time to ask. If you can deliver anything except an ask, you're in the right place.

What's the difference between the two programs?

Wealth Expansion Code is where almost everyone starts: 90 days, the 5 Pillars, and one outcome — you make the ask you've been avoiding. Business Expansion Code is for founders scaling, running the 8 Pillars: delegation, pricing at scale, visibility, team, decisions. Not sure which? That's exactly what the Program Clarity Call is for.

Is this therapy?

No. This is mentoring and coaching. It's not a replacement for medical or psychological care, and if you're working with a therapist, please keep going — this sits alongside that work, never instead of it.

I've tried everything. Why would this be different?

Because everything you've tried talked to your mind. The ask happens in your body — in the second before you speak, your body decides whether it's safe. We work there first. That's why the strategy finally sticks.

How much time does it take?

The work is built for people with real jobs and real businesses. Self-paced modules you can watch anywhere, a live group connect each week, and a daily check-in that takes about two minutes.

What's the guarantee?

In 90 days you'll make the ask you've been avoiding — or we keep working with you until you do. The one condition: you show up and do the work. Programs are non-refundable; instead of refunds, you get our continued commitment until you get there.
